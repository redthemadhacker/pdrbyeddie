How do I set up an appointment?

Setting up an appointment is fast and flexible. You can reach out directly by phone or submit your vehicle details online, and we can usually get you scheduled within a day or two. Before any work begins, we inspect the damage in person and provide a final estimate so you know the exact cost before we start the repair.

  • Call or Text Us Directly: Give us a call or text at 706-888-8625 to speak with us right away and lock in a convenient time.
  • Fill Out Our Quick Online Form: Complete the form right here on our site with your vehicle's Year, Make, and Model, plus a quick description of the dent. We’ll get an email notification immediately, review your details, and follow up to confirm your appointment.
