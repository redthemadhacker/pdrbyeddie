How long can I expect the repair to take?

Most repairs are completed in 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the size, location, and difficulty of the dent. Unlike traditional body shops that keep your car for days or weeks, we get you back on the road the very same day.

  • Fast Turnaround: Minor dings and hail spots are usually fixed in under an hour while you wait.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Appointments are set up at your convenience, usually within a few days of your initial call.
  • Job Difficulty: Larger or deeper dents in hard-to-reach areas might take a bit longer, but we’ll always give you a clear time estimate before starting.
  • Zero Waiting for Paint to Dry: Since there are no fillers, primers, or paint involved, there’s no curing time. As soon as the metal is massaged flat, your car is ready to go.
