How do you remove the dents?

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) is all about using specialized tools and hand technique to physically manipulate metal back into its original factory shape—without damaging the clear coat or paint.

  • Specialized Steel Rods & Picks: We use dozens of custom-forged steel rods in different lengths, bends, and tip styles. These let us reach deep inside door panels, fenders, and roofs to carefully massage the metal out from behind.
  • LED Reflector Boards: To see every detail of the damage, we shine specialized LED boards on the panel. The reflected lines bend around the dent, showing us the exact micro-points that need to be adjusted.
  • Glue-Pulling Systems: When we can't access the back of a panel, we use high-strength pulling tabs attached directly to the exterior. Using specialized lifters or slide hammers, we pull the metal up safely from the outside without touching your paint.
  • Tap-Down & Knock-Down Tools: High spots are just as important as low spots. We use soft-tipped plastic or brass tools to gently tap down high edges, relaxing the tension in the metal so the whole panel flattens back out smoothly.
