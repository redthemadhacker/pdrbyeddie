Is PDR an accepted alternative by the insurance industry?

Yes! Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) is fully accepted by the insurance industry and is actually the preferred method of repair for minor dents, door dings, and hail damage.

  • Industry-Standard Approval: Insurance companies favor PDR because it fixes the actual damage without requiring expensive replacement panels, aftermarket parts, or full repaints.
  • Preserves Vehicle Value: Insurers prefer keeping your original factory paint intact because it prevents color-mismatch claims and preserves your car’s total resale value.
  • Lower Repair Costs: Because PDR eliminates expensive paint materials, booth fees, and heavy shop labor, total claim costs stay lower—which helps keep your rates manageable.
  • Faster Claims & Turnaround: Insurance repairs often take weeks at traditional body shops. PDR gets claims approved and repairs finished in a fraction of the time, saving insurance companies on rental car coverage.
