What is paintless dent repair?

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) is the fastest, cleanest way to get rid of dents, dings, and hail damage without messing up your car's original factory paint. Instead of slapping body filler over the damage or spending thousands on a fresh paint job, we use specialized tools to carefully massage the metal back into its original shape from the inside out.

  • Keeps Your Factory Paint: No repainting means zero risk of mismatched colors or peeling down the road. Your car keeps its original finish and its resale value.
  • Saves You Money: Without the high cost of paint, clear coat, and heavy shop labor, repairs stay affordable.
  • Back on the Road Fast: Most repairs take just a couple of hours—not days or weeks like traditional body shops.
  • Zero Chemicals or Fillers: It’s a clean, eco-friendly process that fixes the actual metal rather than just hiding the damage.
