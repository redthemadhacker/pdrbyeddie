How much does an average paintless dent repair cost?

The average retail dent repair costs between $75 and $150, depending on the size, location, and severity of the dent. Compared to a traditional body shop, PDR is significantly more affordable because you aren't paying for extra materials like filler, primer, or costly paint.

  • Size of the Dent: Minor door dings and hail spots stay on the lower end ($75–$150), while larger crease dents require more time and precision tools.
  • Location on the Car: Dents on open panels (like doors or fenders) are quick to reach. If a dent is sitting right along a reinforced crease or under tight bracing, it takes extra technique to massage back out.
  • Multiple Dents: If you have several small dings or hail damage across a panel, repairing them all in one visit saves you money compared to fixing them individually.
  • No Hidden Fees: Because there is zero sanding or repainting involved, you don't have to worry about added costs for paint matching or booth time.
